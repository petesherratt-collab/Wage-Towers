# Conditional monitoring pilot design v0.2

25 November 2026. Design only, prepared after the v0.2 synthetic evaluation. No firms selected, collection started, archives changed, automation scheduled or findings published.

## Purpose and entry conditions

Test whether the required evidence can be obtained and consistently assessed, not estimate AI displacement prevalence. The synthetic tests establish useful measurement distinctions but retain hidden-confounding false positives, imposed economic responses and severe missing-evidence limitations. Consequently a pilot must be an evidence-feasibility exercise with explicitly conditional attribution.

Before launch: freeze the candidate rules and evidence dictionary; fix cumulative-flow windows following the next synthetic experiment; test field-specific delays, revisions, attrition and selective pay-panel exits; prepare a versioned local evidence destination explicitly authorised by the user. Existing evidence-archive, evidence-control and evidence-archive-snapshot remain outside the authorised changes. Starting monitoring requires a further explicit instruction under AGENTS.md; no approval is requested merely to complete this design.

## Small proposed sample and duration

Use four adopting organisation-functions across two task families, each paired with one comparison function: eight units in total. These counts are workload choices, not a powered statistical sample. Select from a declared sampling frame by evidence availability, operational comparability and timing, not by a layoff headline. Preserve an inclusion/exclusion log so favourable disclosure is visible. Seek both expansion and contraction contexts. If credible comparisons are unavailable, retain cases as Level 1/feasibility cases rather than invent a counterfactual.

Use three or more historical pre-adoption quarters and up to eight post-adoption quarters. Start with a retrospective, publication-date replay; only then consider a twelve-month prospective phase with quarterly assessments and a limited monthly check for newly published records. Reporting delays may mean the prospective phase ends with censored cases. This cadence is a proposal, not a scheduled task.

## Record schema and workflow

For each source store source ID/URL, origin and independence, publication and event dates, retrieval date/version, passage or table, extraction method, units, boundary, missing fields, revisions and assessor judgement. Retain original and revised assessments separately; never backfill information into earlier as-of-date decisions.

For each unit record task execution and effort, quality, task mix, employee/contractor hours, headcount, hires/exits/transfers and reasons, hourly pay, paid hours, annual earnings, pay components/deflator, composition, output/demand, adoption timing, restructuring decisions and comparator exposure. Do not substitute vacancies, company claims or disappearing web pages for workforce observations. Contractor and within-grade pay coverage may be unobtainable publicly; treat that as a feasibility finding.

Apply T, H and W separately. F remains experimental until the flow-window issue is resolved. Do not score P without allocation/value evidence. For each case publish internally a result tuple: signal/magnitude; adequacy/missingness; attribution/alternatives; latest observed period; assessment date. Use “conditional support” with named assumptions, never a count of proven AI job losses. Any wider publication requires separate authorisation.

Propose two independent human assessments of each packet, initially masked to the company's causal narrative; record disagreements and reconcile them against the same rulebook. This is a future protocol, not a claim that independent review occurred here. A small pilot cannot generally observe causal truth, so disagreement and later corroboration are not substitutes for measured sensitivity/specificity.

## Evaluation and stop rules

Track fields available, timeliness, missing contractor/pay coverage, percentage of assessable units, time spent per case, reviewer agreement, revisions, contradictory evidence, alerts later withdrawn and reasons. Report numerators and denominators. Preserve all non-alerts and unresolved cases to expose collection bias.

Provisional feasibility gate: at least three of four adopting units have task execution plus boundary-consistent hours for two post-adoption periods, and at least two have comparable-pay evidence. These are pragmatic workload gates, not validation thresholds. Failure should trigger a smaller claim or a revised observation design, not relaxed causal rules. Stop extending the pilot if observation burden prevents maintaining versioned source records, or if workforce boundaries cannot be reconciled; report what remains unknown.

Deliverable after retrospective replay: eight unit-level evidence dossiers, an exclusion/missingness ledger, a reproducible assessment file and a feasibility decision. Proceed to prospective monitoring only after reviewing this result and receiving explicit launch instruction. No named watchlist is needed until that point.
