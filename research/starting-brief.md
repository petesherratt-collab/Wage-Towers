# Starting brief

Established 24 November 2026 from the user's discovery answers, the original chat's recent research record, saved artefacts and read-only inspection of the live prototype.

## Agreed direction
Codex researches and implements. Experiments and simulation take priority; accepted challenges become experiments or tests of the simulation itself. The wage-support hypothesis is open to rejection. Keep job losses, suppressed hiring, reduced hours and wage erosion separate, including counterfactual reductions despite growth.

The first milestone is a portfolio of candidate indicators with required observations, causal assumptions, uncertainty states and synthetic tests. Definitions and attribution rules support that milestone. Collection repositories, monitoring and publication require further explicit instruction.

Prototype: https://wage-towers-lab-241126.petesherratt.chatgpt.site/

The live interface was inspected on 24 November 2026. It describes ten initial role slots, a $1 million starting annual payroll and 20 quarters. Each brick represents $1,000 annual wages. Modes cover constant AI, automation only, protected new work, AI pursuit and learning feedback, with controls, repeated seeds and an assumption ledger.

Displayed limitations include interchangeable workers, a 30-role ceiling, nominal wages, no economy-wide spending feedback or unemployment duration, and no empirical calibration. Role slots do not track individual people. New-work revenue and learning assumptions are deliberately favourable in some respects. Wage pressure is imposed rather than derived from bargaining. These are research targets, not accepted facts.

## Synthetic benchmark v0.1
Read outputs/synthetic-indicator/Findings-and-rules.md for the original rules and findings. It reports 16 artificial histories and 640 evidence-loss variations, with observation packets separated from causal truth. These are designed cases, not an independent validation or true holdout.

Its illustrative rule compares changes in human labour intensity and real pay with an unexposed comparator, using a strictly greater than 10% threshold. Neither the cutoff nor proportional output adjustment is empirically established. Do not silently promote them into project requirements.

Preserve the identical-observation pair with opposite causal truths and the deceptive comparator case producing a false positive. They demonstrate non-identifiability and hidden-confounding risk. The initial benchmark uses two periods and cannot estimate detection time.

## First milestone acceptance
Deliver distinct candidate specifications and a comparison, with explicit tested/untested status. Each needs an observable definition, evidence-adequacy judgement, attribution judgement, failure cases and reproducible synthetic test design. Cover employment quantity and wage erosion; distinguish pressure from realised consequences. Identify additional observations needed to distinguish ambiguous histories.

Real-world rates, economic validation and monitoring remain future work.
